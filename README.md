# The Aggregate Miss Ratio Can Hide Tenant Harm

Research materials for the paper **“The Aggregate Miss Ratio Can Hide Tenant Harm: Reviewing and Testing How Page-Cache Replacement Is Evaluated.”**

## Files

- [Final research paper (PDF)](Final_Research_Paper_v5.pdf)
- [Research materials and project archive (ZIP)](page-cache-tenant-harm.zip)

The paper combines a structured narrative review of page-cache and cache-replacement evaluation practices with simulation studies and a Linux memory-cgroup experiment. It examines how aggregate cache miss ratios can conceal adverse outcomes for individual workloads sharing a cache.

## Authors

Dhanush R, Thrupthi G D, Rohan K, and Rohan S  
Department of Computer Science and Engineering (AI&ML), PES University, Bengaluru, India.
